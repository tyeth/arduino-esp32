Metro ESP32-P4 TinyUF2 images

These images use ESP-IDF v6.1 and target pre-v3 ESP32-P4 silicon (image revision
range 0.1 through 1.99). For newer silicon, select the standard Default partition
scheme until matching TinyUF2 images are available.

  idf.py -B build-metro-p4 -DBOARD=adafruit_metro_esp32p4 build

The TinyUF2 partition scheme installs both images during an ordinary Arduino
upload and includes them in the merged flash image and generated flash_args.
It provides two 4 MB application slots and 7872 KB of FAT storage.

Double-reset entry requires CBATRAW = 2.2 uF on the GPIO18 battery divider.
Tap RESET again during the dim purple window to open METROP4BOOT. With the
original capacitor, tap RESET, then press BOOT during the purple window instead.
Holding BOOT during RESET still enters the independent ROM downloader.
